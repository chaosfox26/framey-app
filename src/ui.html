Before you start, on the headset:

  1. Steam Settings > System > Enable Developer Mode.
  2. Developer (left menu) > scroll to the bottom > Set User Password.
  3. Keep the headset awake and on the same network as this computer.

Then enter its address and password and click Install. The password is needed the first time and for Fan Control, and is never saved.



Close this tab and the app window to quit, or quit now.
